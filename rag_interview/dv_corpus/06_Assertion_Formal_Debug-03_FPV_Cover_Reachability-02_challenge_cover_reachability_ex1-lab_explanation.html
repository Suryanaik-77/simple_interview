🧭 What this challenge is

The same cover target (count==10), but the counter wraps to 0 at 8, so 10 is never reached.

💡 The property being violated

The cover property asks formal to prove the scenario count==10 is reachable; a hit produces a witness trace, a miss (unreachable) means dead logic or an over-constrained environment.

cover property (count == 10);   // must be reachable - JasperGold finds a witness trace
🛠️ Debugging Guide
🔴 Symptom - what JasperGold reports

JasperGold reports the cover c_reach10 as unreachable - no trace in the whole state space reaches count==10.

🔎 How to investigate
  1. Note the cover is unreachable, not a cex.
  2. Open fpv_cover.sv and read the wrap condition.
  3. Trace the reachable values: the counter never gets to 10.
🧭 Fix direction

Remove the early wrap so the counter can advance past 8 up to at least 10 (let it free-run or wrap at 15).

✅ How to confirm your fix

Re-run ./run.sh; JasperGold should report the property proven (or the cover covered) with no counterexample, and the lab ends with RESULT: TEST PASSED.
