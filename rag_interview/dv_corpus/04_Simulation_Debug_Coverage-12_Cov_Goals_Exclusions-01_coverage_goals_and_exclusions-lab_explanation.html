🧭 What this lab is

A design exercised so that VCS structural coverage (-cm line+cond+branch) reaches the goal. The coverage SCORE reported by urg is the pass/fail verdict - a hole drops it below the goal.

⚙️ How it runs

VCS compiles and simulates with -cm to record coverage into simv.vdb; urg then generates urgReport/dashboard.txt. run.sh parses the overall SCORE and compares it to the 99.99% goal.

🎯 What you'll learn
  • Collect structural coverage with VCS -cm
  • Generate and read a urg coverage report
  • Recognize a coverage hole and decide fix-vs-exclude
