Golden Example
🧭 What this lab is

The reference lab builds an env that instantiates a my_agent (with driver, sequencer, monitor) and three independent subscribers — func_cov, scoreboard, and statistics — all fed from the same monitor analysis_port to demonstrate the one-to-many broadcast pattern.

💡 The concept

TLM analysis ports decouple producers from consumers: a uvm_analysis_port can be connect()-ed to any number of analysis_exports, and calling write() on the port invokes write() on every connected subscriber in the order they were connected. This means the monitor never needs to know how many things are watching, and each subscriber (func_cov, scoreboard, statistics) implements its own write() to do its own job — sampling a covergroup, checking a result, or tallying counts — completely independently of the others.

⚙️ How it works

my_agent::build_phase creates drv (a driver extending uvm_driver #(seq_item)), seqr, and mon as an active agent, then connect_phase wires drv.seq_item_port to seqr.seq_item_export so the driver can pull sequence items. driver::run_phase loops forever calling seq_item_port.get_next_item(req), driving it in drive_item() (logging addr/data/write/burst_type and consuming #10), then seq_item_port.item_done(). At the env level, env::build_phase creates agt (my_agent) plus the three subscribers fc (func_cov), sb (scoreboard), and stats (statistics). env::connect_phase then connects the agent's monitor analysis port to all three subscribers' analysis_exports, so a single mon.analysis_port.write(txn) call fans out to all three write() implementations simultaneously — illustrating the broadcast architecture called out in the file's header comment.

🎯 What you'll learn
  • How to declare and instantiate uvm_subscriber #(T) components and override their write(T t) method
  • How uvm_analysis_port.connect() fans a single publisher out to multiple analysis_export consumers
  • How to wire agent-to-environment analysis connections inside connect_phase rather than build_phase
  • Why passive analysis components (coverage/scoreboard/statistics) stay decoupled from the driver/sequencer path
⚠️ Common mistakes
  • Forgetting to call connect() on the analysis port in connect_phase, so the subscriber's write() is never invoked even though it compiles and elaborates cleanly
  • Doing analysis-port connect() calls in build_phase instead of connect_phase, before the target components' exports exist/are ready to bind
