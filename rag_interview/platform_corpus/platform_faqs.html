Dashboard & Navigation

How do I move between Home, Skills, Competencies, Journey, and Labs?

You can see Home, Skills, Journey & Profile on the header bar.

What is the difference between Skills and Competencies?

Skills are certification paths made up of Competencies, Lab Testcases, and a Quiz. Once you complete all of these, you earn a certificate for that skill. If you complete only a Competency, you cannot earn a certificate.

How do I switch my active tool?

Switching tools is only available for Pro users. If you are a Pro user, you can switch the tool from the top-left corner.

Plans & Subscriptions

What is included in Basic vs Pro plans?

In the Basic plan, you can only work with one tool. In the Pro plan, you can switch tools and handle bigger projects/labs.

Can I upgrade from Basic to Pro?

Yes, you can upgrade from Basic to Pro in the Profile section.

Domains & EDA Tools

Can I switch my EDA tool on the Pro plan?

Yes, you can switch your EDA tool on the Pro plan.

Skills & Certifications

How do I enroll in a skill?

On the Home page, you can see the list of skills available to you. Open a skill to preview what's inside, then enroll.

Where can I see skills I am enrolled in?

You can see your enrolled skills on the main Home page, along with all the analytics for that skill.

Where can I see all the Theoretical Documents?

You can find all the Documents related to your domain in the Knowledge Base which is available on the header section.

How can I get a Certificate?

You can earn a certificate only after completing all the Competencies in your enrolled skill.

Competencies & Learning

How do I open a competency?

After enrolling in a skill, you can click Continue Learning on the Home page, or click the Skills button in the header section.

What content is inside a competency?

The content inside the Competencies are Objectives, Core Concepts, Lab Guide, Success Criteria and a Small Quiz.

Why are labs in a competency locked?

It means you haven't enrolled in that Competency/Skill. You need to enroll first before you can see all the labs in it.

Labs & Practicals

How do I start a lab?

Before starting the lab, you need to go through all the lab setup instructions available at the top, then click the Start Lab button.

How long does lab provisioning take?

It takes 5-10 minutes to set up the lab for the first time.

Can I stop a lab and resume later?

Yes, you can stop the lab once you've completed your work and resume it again whenever needed.

How can I login to the DCV Environment?

You can use the Application Credentials for the DCV Environment. If you are still facing any issue please raise a ticket in support section.

Final Exam & Certificates

When can I take the Quiz?

Once you clear all the Labs in every Competency you've enrolled in, your Quiz gets unlocked.

How do I download my certificate?

Once you clear all the Competencies in the Skill, you can download your certificate from the Journey page or the Profile section.

Can I add my certificate to LinkedIn?

Yes, you can add your certificate to your LinkedIn profile.

Payments & Renewal

Who can purchase or renew for a team account?

A group of at least 2 people is eligible for Teams. For more information, visit SemiconLabs.com.

Data Backup

What is optional data backup?

Once you've used up the lab hours you purchased, the data backup stores your work until the data backup plan expires. This does not extend your lab hours - it only preserves your data. To continue working on the labs, you'll need to purchase the plan again.

Account & Security

How do I reset my password?

You can reset your password in the Profile section, available in the top-right corner of the header.

How do I update my profile photo?

You can update your profile photo in the Profile section, available in the top-right corner of the header.

How do I update my LinkedIn profile URL?

You can update your LinkedIn URL in the Profile section, available in the top-right corner of the header.
