INDEPENDENT WORK

You are tasked with synthesizing a Tile design. The initial synthesis attempt reveals numerous unresolved references.

Your Challenge: Fix all linking issues and achieve successful synthesis of the complete tile design with zero warnings.

⚠️ Complexity Warning: This testcase is significantly more complex than the previous ones. You may need to iterate multiple times, fixing issues layer by layer as deeper dependencies are revealed.

📋 Requirements & Deliverables

Resolve Linking & Environment Dependencies

  • Locate all missing source files causing LINK-5 warnings.
  • Ensure that the link_library variable in your synthesis script correctly references the required memory macro and standard cell databases (.db files).
  • Update the synthesis script to include all necessary files.
  • Ensure the design links successfully with zero LINK-5 warnings and no unresolved reference errors.
