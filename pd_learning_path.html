Why this order

Stage 1 · Synthesis

Everything in physical design starts here. You cannot place and route a design that has not been synthesized into a gate-level netlist, so Synthesis is the entry point for every other stage in this course.

Stage 2 · LEC

Immediately after synthesis, verify that the netlist is functionally identical to the RTL. Catching equivalence failures before committing to layout is far cheaper than finding them after tape-out. LEC is placed here because it is a natural synthesis checkpoint — but it is not a hard prerequisite for PnR. See the flexible paths below.

Stage 3 · PnR

The core of physical design. Modules are sequenced to mirror the actual PnR flow: floorplan before placement, placement before routing, routing before chip finish. The stage order within PnR is fixed; the entry point into the course is not.

Stage 4 · PV

Physical verification operates on a completed, routed layout — it has no meaning earlier in the flow. Calibre is used here because it is the industry sign-off standard, independent of which PnR tool produced the layout.

Stage 5 · STA

Full static timing analysis — especially multi-corner DMSA — requires real post-route parasitics from the finished layout. Running STA last means the timing numbers are accurate and the ECOs generated are directly implementable.

Catalog at a glance — how many labs are available

There are 22 lab modules available now across the five stages. Synthesis, PnR and STA are each offered in both Synopsys and Cadence (your choice of tool), so each of those modules is two separate labs — one per tool. LEC is Synopsys (Formality) only for now, and PV uses Calibre for every student. Counting each tool's version as its own lab, that is 35 labs available now.

Where the labs live on disk — lab directory paths

Every lab lives under ~/pd/labs/. A lab's directory is named <STAGE>-<TOOL>-<MODULE>, where TOOL is SNPS (Synopsys), CDN (Cadence) or SIE (Siemens Calibre). Inside each lab directory are its testcase folders: the Guided testcase TC-<STAGE>-<TOOL>-<MODULE>-GD-001 and the Challenge testcase TC-<STAGE>-<TOOL>-<MODULE>-CH-001. So a full path looks like ~/pd/labs/PNR-SNPS-DI/TC-PNR-SNPS-DI-GD-001 (Synopsys Design-Init guided lab). To open a lab you cd into its testcase folder.

Stage 4 — PV (Calibre, tool code SIE): DRC Flow: ~/pd/labs/PV-SIE-DRC/TC-PV-SIE-DRC-GD-001. LVS Flow: ~/pd/labs/PV-SIE-LVS/TC-PV-SIE-LVS-GD-001. Antenna: ~/pd/labs/PV-SIE-ANT/TC-PV-SIE-ANT-GD-001. Dummy Fill FEOL: ~/pd/labs/PV-SIE-DF_FEOL/TC-PV-SIE-DF_FEOL-GD-001. Dummy Fill BEOL: ~/pd/labs/PV-SIE-DF_BEOL/TC-PV-SIE-DF_BEOL-GD-001. GDS Merging: ~/pd/labs/PV-SIE-GDS/TC-PV-SIE-GDS-GD-001. (PV is Calibre-only, so there is no SNPS/CDN variant and only a Guided testcase.)

Stage 1 — Synthesis module codes (each with SNPS and CDN dirs): Unresolved References URR, Check Timing Issues CT, Logic Aware Synthesis LS, Physical Aware Synthesis PS — e.g. ~/pd/labs/SYN-SNPS-URR/TC-SYN-SNPS-URR-GD-001.

Time estimates — how long does each lab take

Each module has two lab variants: a Guided lab (step-by-step with checkpoints, shorter) and a Challenge lab (open-ended, minimal hints, longer). Estimates below are per-module averages across both tool choices (Synopsys and Cadence times are similar for most modules).

Stage 1 — Synthesis (4 modules): Unresolved References: Guided 45–60 min, Challenge 90–120 min. Check Timing Issues: Guided 45–60 min, Challenge 90–120 min. Logic Aware Synthesis: Guided 60–90 min, Challenge 90–120 min. Physical Aware Synthesis: Guided 90–120 min, Challenge 90–120 min.

Stage 2 — LEC (3 modules, Synopsys Formality): RTL to Netlist: Guided 45–60 min, Challenge 90–120 min. Netlist to Netlist: Guided 45–60 min, Challenge 90–120 min. Clock Gating App Option: Guided 60–90 min, Challenge 60 min.

Stage 3 — PnR (7 modules): Design Init: Guided 60–90 min, Challenge 90–120 min. Floorplan: Guided 60–90 min, Challenge 90–120 min. Placement: Guided 60–90 min, Challenge 90–120 min. CTS: Guided 60–90 min, Challenge 90–120 min. Route: Guided 120–150 min, Challenge 90–150 min. Post Route: Guided 60–90 min, Challenge 90–120 min. Chip Finish: Guided 60–90 min, Challenge 90–120 min.

Stage 4 — PV (6 modules, Calibre): DRC Flow: 20 min. LVS Flow: 30 min. Antenna: 20 min. Dummy Fill FEOL: 20 min. Dummy Fill BEOL: 20 min. GDS Merging: 20 min. PV labs are shorter because they run on a pre-completed routed layout.

Stage 5 — STA (2 modules): STA Flow: Guided 1.5–2 hours, Challenge 1.5–6 hours (Synopsys STA is longer due to full multi-corner setup). DMSA Based ECO Generation: Guided 60–90 min, Challenge 60–90 min.

Overall: plan for 1–2 hours per Guided lab and 1.5–3 hours per Challenge lab, except PV labs which are 20–30 minutes each. Total course time across all 35 labs is approximately 60–80 hours depending on tool choice and experience level.
